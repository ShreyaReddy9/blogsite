Welcome to my blog...

I completed my Master’s in Computer Science from San Diego State University. I discovered my passion for Computer Science in a fun and unexpected way — through a YouTube video that piqued my curiosity about how software works and what a day in the life of a software developer looks like. That moment sparked a journey of exploration, learning, and eventually building real-world applications myself.

Here, I share what I’ve been learning in the world of technology — from programming and new tools to emerging trends — along with fascinating psychology insights and thought-provoking articles I come across. It’s a space where curiosity meets creativity, and I hope you enjoy exploring both sides with me.

When I'm not diving into code or exploring the human mind, you'll find me with a paintbrush in hand, lost in a swirl of colors and imagination. Art is my sanctuary — a space where creativity flows freely and stories come to life.
